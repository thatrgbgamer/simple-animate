          About

          Simple Animate

          A 2D frame-by-frame animation studio. Draw, flip, and export a real video file — nothing else to install.

          • Adjustable onion skin: frames before and after, opacity, falloff, red/blue tint.
          • Brush, eraser, line, rectangle, ellipse, triangle, polygon, paint bucket, eyedropper.
          • Hex colour picker with saturation/value field, hue slider and recent swatches.
          • Any canvas resolution, resizable at any time, with artwork scaling.
          • Export to ProRes .mov (best quality, alpha optional), H.264 .mp4, or a PNG sequence.
